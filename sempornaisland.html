Semporna Island, Sabah

Semporna, located on the east coast of Sabah, is the main gateway to some of the world's best island resorts and diving sites. Known for its crystal clear blue waters and stunning coral reefs, this destination offers a tropical paradise on par with the Maldives, perfect for family vacations and scuba divers. Semporna is also known for the Regatta Lepa traditional boat races which occur annually in April. Semporna was also the location of the finish line of Eco-Challenge: Borneo, held in 2000. Off the coast is a marine park called Tun Sakaran Marine Park, also known as Semporna Islands Park. It was gazetted by Sabah Parks in 2004. Visitors to Semporna are mainly sunseekers looking for relaxation or watersports activities such as scuba diving or snorkelling.

Activities

Scuba Diving

Discover the vibrant underwater world on a scuba dive day trip with an eco-conscious operator. Scuba Dive in stunning turquoise waters among the beautiful hard and soft corals, colorful reefs teeming with tropical fish.

Jet Skiing

Enjoy water sports and feel the thrill of jet skiing. Explore the beauty of Lover’s Bay on Timbun Mata Island. Board a boat and set off for Lover’s Bay on Timbun Mata Island.

Fishing

Embark on a half-day deep sea fishing trip from Semporna. Learn fishing techniques from a guide, enjoy the ocean views, and try your luck at catching local fish species.

ATV Adventure

Feel the thrill of off-roading through the jungle on a single-rider ATV. Gear up with a safety helmet and hop onto your exclusive single-rider ATV for a thrilling 15-20 minute ride through local jungle trails.
