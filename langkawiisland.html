Langkawi Island, Kedah

Langkawi, officially known as Langkawi, the Jewel of Kedah (Malay: Langkawi Permata Kedah), is a duty-free island and an archipelago of 99 islands. The name Langkawi is thought to have existed by the early 15th century, although in the 16th century the island of Langkawi was also marked on maps variously as Langa, Langka, Lansura, and Langapura. Langkawi is an archipelago district in the state of Kedah Darul Aman, Malaysia. Today, this island, rich in natural beauty and legends of the past, has emerged as one of the most popular tourist destinations in Malaysia. Langkawi is an island cluster consisting of 99 islands, of which Pulau Langkawi is the largest. Langkawi Island is known as the Jewel of Kedah and offers beautiful scenery. Langkawi is the perfect place to relax your mind after a tiring day at work for those who want to escape the hustle and bustle of the city. Enjoy nature-based activities, or relax by the beach.

Activities

Dataran Lang

An icon of Langkawi, Dataran Lang is the largest sculpture of the Eagle bird which is the origin of Langkawi's name, Helang and Kawi. It is one of the best places for photography by Langkawi visitors.

Langkawi Sky Bridge

Spanning 125 metres across the Langkawi skyline, the SkyBridge is not just a bridge. It is a world-class engineering marvel and one of the longest curved suspension bridges on the planet, offering more than just breathtaking views.

Temurun Waterfall

Temurun Waterfall located in Datai Bay is the tallest 3-tiered waterfall in Langkawi. This beautiful waterfall is fed by river water from the north of Gunung Mat Cincang.

Langkawi Gliders

Experience the beauty of Langkawi from above with a thrilling gliding adventure. Enjoy breathtaking views of the island, coastline and surrounding landscapes from the sky.
